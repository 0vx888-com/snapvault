FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is SnapVault?

A creator social and paid-content app built on X Layer. Creators share free Stories; fans unlock paid content with OKB.

How do I start?

Go to https://www.snapvault.cc/ and connect your wallet to browse, follow creators, and unlock content.

What do I pay with?

OKB, on X Layer.

What is the platform fee, and how do creators get paid?

The configured platform fee is 10%; the current contract rate shown in the app applies. The rest is credited to the creator, who can withdraw it directly in the app. Network gas is charged separately.

What are Stories?

Free photo posts shown for 24 hours, for daily moments, works in progress, and previews. Photos only for now (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, up to 2 MB); video is on the roadmap.

How is the 24-hour Story different from timed viewing?

24 hours is how long a Story stays visible. Timed viewing is a countdown after you open paid content, such as 10 or 60 seconds. They are separate features.

Does timed viewing stop screenshots?

No. It limits viewing inside the app. It cannot stop screenshots or screen recording, and it does not mean the file is permanently erased.

Can I add friends and chat?

Yes. Add friends by UID or wallet address and send text and photo messages. Free text and photo messages in private and group chats are synced by the platform service. Senders can set a viewing timer.

Are there group chats?

Group chats support text, photos, and paid-photo cards. Each member buys a paid photo separately; paying to join a group or recurring fan subscriptions is not supported.

Is SnapVault fully decentralized?

No. Payments, splits, and withdrawals run on X Layer smart contracts and the frontend is hosted on TapeOut DeWEB, but parts of the backend and content storage are centralized.

Which on-chain and TapeOut capabilities does SnapVault use?

Payments, splits, and withdrawals run on a verified, non-upgradeable smart contract on X Layer mainnet (chain 196): 0xFd9b25442955C09007abB2D10dEcD24b4d2CF83f. The frontend is hosted on TapeOut DeWEB. For paid photos, TapeSend delivers viewing links to buyers with a supported container and public key; other buyers receive them in the app.

Is there an English version?

Yes, the interface switches between English and Chinese.

Is the code open source?

The code is on GitHub: https://github.com/0vx888-com/snapvault

常见问题

常见问题

SnapVault 是什么？

一个构建在 X Layer 上的创作者社交与付费内容应用。创作者用免费 Story 分享日常，粉丝用 OKB 解锁付费内容。

怎么开始使用？

打开 https://www.snapvault.cc/ ，连接钱包登录即可浏览、关注创作者和解锁内容。

用什么付款？

用 OKB，在 X Layer 链上付款。

平台抽成多少？创作者怎么拿到钱？

平台配置费率为 10%，以页面显示的当前合约费率为准；其余计入创作者收益，由创作者在产品里自行提现。网络 gas 另计。

Story 是什么？

免费的图片动态，发布后展示 24 小时，适合分享日常、创作过程和作品预告。目前只支持图片（PNG、JPEG、WebP、GIF，最大 2 MB），视频在后续计划中。

Story 的 24 小时和付费内容的限时查看有什么区别？

24 小时是 Story 的展示有效期。限时查看是付费内容打开后的观看倒计时，例如 10 秒或 60 秒。两者是不同机制。

限时查看能防止截图吗？

不能。限时查看控制的是产品内的观看权限，无法阻止截图、录屏或拍摄，也不代表文件被彻底删除。

可以加好友和聊天吗？

可以。通过 UID 或钱包地址加好友，私聊和群聊支持免费文字与图片，由平台服务同步。发送者可设置阅后即焚时长。

有群聊吗？

支持群聊文字、图片和付费图片卡片。群成员分别购买图片；付费入群和周期订阅尚不支持。

SnapVault 是完全去中心化的吗？

不是。支付、分账和提现在 X Layer 智能合约上完成，前端部署在 TapeOut DeWEB 上，但后端和内容存储仍有中心化部分。

SnapVault 用到了哪些链上和 TapeOut 能力？

付款、分账和提现由 X Layer 主网（chain 196）上已开源验证、不可升级的智能合约处理，合约地址为 0xFd9b25442955C09007abB2D10dEcD24b4d2CF83f。前端部署在 TapeOut 的 DeWEB 上。付费图片向已有可用容器和公钥的买家通过 TapeSend 交付查看链接，其他买家使用站内交付。

支持英文吗？

支持，界面可以在中文和英文之间切换。

代码开源吗？

项目代码在 GitHub：https://github.com/0vx888-com/snapvault
